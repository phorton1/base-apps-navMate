Welcome aboard. This manual walks you through installing navMate, connecting your computer to
your Raymarine E80, and using navMate to manage your waypoints, routes, and tracks.

navMate is the home for your navigation data across a lifetime of sailing -- not a companion to
any single chartplotter, but the place all of it lives. You plan on your computer, send what you
need out to the boat, and bring your tracks and changes back when you return to the dock.
